# Capabilities and development direction

Vision CoDev delivers an end-to-end visual guidance workflow for capturing,
reviewing, and reusing expert knowledge during physical work.

## Current capabilities

### Golden Run capture

An expert demonstration becomes the evidence source for a reusable procedure.
The capture workflow connects the physical recording to the later review and
guidance experience.

### Evidence-linked procedure creation

Vision CoDev structures a demonstration into clear procedure steps. Each step
can be reviewed against the relevant moment in the original evidence instead of
existing as disconnected generated text.

### Expert review and controlled approval

Authorized reviewers correct the proposed procedure, approve its content, and
publish a controlled version for use. Human approval remains a core part of the
workflow.

### Live worker guidance

During task execution, Vision CoDev delivers the current instruction through
visual and spoken guidance. The system follows the approved sequence and keeps
the worker focused on the step that matters now.

### Deviation and uncertainty handling

Vision CoDev distinguishes normal progress from selected observable deviations
and uncertain conditions. It can clarify the current step, wait for better
evidence, request confirmation, offer a reviewed recovery, or escalate when the
approved procedure does not cover the situation.

### Procedure improvement

Reviewed session outcomes show where instructions, demonstrations, or recovery
paths should improve. Updates create a clearer procedure rather than silently
changing the one already in use.

## Complete workflow

`capture → structure → review → approve → guide → detect → recover`

The value comes from the complete loop: recorded expertise becomes approved
guidance, and live guidance stays connected to that approved source.

## Development direction

Ongoing product development expands the range of supported procedures and
operating environments while strengthening:

- onboarding for additional workflows;
- procedure versioning and approval controls;
- evaluation across people, equipment, and environments;
- deployment, access, retention, and audit controls;
- organizational integrations and reporting; and
- support for additional capture sources and multi-stage work.

## Deployment-specific performance

Visual guidance performance is specific to the procedure and conditions in
which it operates. Camera placement, lighting, equipment variants, obstructions,
connectivity, and user behavior all matter. Vision CoDev deployments should be
evaluated against representative recordings and measurable task outcomes for
the intended environment.
