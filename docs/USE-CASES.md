# Illustrative use cases

These examples show how Vision CoDev applies to common hands-on workflows.
Suitability still depends on the procedure, environment, and required controls.

## Equipment setup

An experienced technician demonstrates the approved setup sequence for a piece
of equipment. A new technician can later follow the reviewed procedure while
receiving reminders about order, alignment, and visible completion checks.

**Operational value:** faster onboarding, fewer missed steps, and more consistent
setup across locations.

## Maintenance and field service

A senior engineer records a repeatable inspection or replacement procedure.
Field personnel can receive context-specific guidance and escalate when the
observed condition is outside the approved procedure.

**Operational value:** wider access to scarce expertise and better consistency in
distributed service operations.

## Assembly and quality work

An approved assembly is captured as a Golden Run. During later work, guidance
can help the operator maintain the intended sequence and recognize selected
observable deviations.

**Operational value:** reduced variation, clearer training, and evidence for
continuous improvement.

## Laboratory or technical procedures

A qualified user records a controlled, non-safety-critical workflow. Reviewed
visual instructions can supplement existing protocols for users who are still
learning the physical sequence.

**Operational value:** easier knowledge transfer and a stronger connection
between written protocols and physical execution.

## Choosing an initial evaluation

A strong first workflow is:

- repeatable and reasonably short;
- visually observable;
- performed often enough to gather representative examples;
- supported by an available subject-matter expert;
- measurable with a clear definition of success; and
- low enough in risk that the assistive workflow can be deployed responsibly.

A poor first workflow depends heavily on invisible internal states, rare events,
uncontrolled environments, or immediate safety-critical decisions.
