# Frequently asked questions

## What is Vision CoDev?

Vision CoDev is a visual guidance platform that captures expert physical work as
a reviewable Golden Run and uses the approved procedure to assist another
person during the task.

## Is it simply a video training library?

No. Video is the evidence foundation, but the intended experience adds
structured steps, expert review, controlled versions, live progress guidance,
and conservative handling of uncertainty or selected deviations.

## Does artificial intelligence write the official procedure?

It can propose structure and wording. A responsible human reviewer decides what
becomes approved. The product principle is that automation assists the expert;
it does not silently replace the expert's authority.

## Does it recognize every possible mistake?

No. Recognition is limited by the approved procedure, available evidence,
tested conditions, camera view, and system capability. Unfamiliar or ambiguous
states should lead to uncertainty handling or escalation rather than invented
certainty.

## Can it be used for safety-critical work?

It must not be the sole control for safety-critical work or replace required
training and procedures. Any higher-risk evaluation needs its own hazard
analysis, safeguards, validation, and qualified oversight.

## What is a Golden Run?

A Golden Run is a reviewed recording of an experienced person completing a
procedure correctly. It anchors the proposed instructions in real evidence.

## Does Vision CoDev work today?

Yes. Vision CoDev implements the complete Golden Run workflow from expert
capture and evidence-linked review through live worker guidance, deviation
detection, and reviewed recovery. Each deployment still needs workflow-specific
configuration, evaluation, and appropriate operational safeguards.

## Why isn't the source code in this repository?

This is a public information repository. The implementation is proprietary and
maintained privately so the project can explain the vision openly without
publishing its source code, infrastructure, security details, or internal
research.

## Can I contribute code here?

No. This repository is for public product documentation and discussion, not
source-code contributions. Feedback about the problem, use cases, terminology,
and evaluation approach is welcome.

## How can an organization explore a pilot?

Start with one short, repeatable, visually observable, lower-risk procedure and
a subject-matter expert who can define success. Open a Discussion or contact the
project owner through their GitHub profile to explore an evaluation.
